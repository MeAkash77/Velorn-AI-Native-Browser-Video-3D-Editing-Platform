THE PRODUCT

AI generation meets a real editor.

Velorn is designed around the complete creative loop: create an idea, generate media, place it on a timeline, refine it, automate supported changes, and deliver the result.

✦

Generate inside the project

Launch documented AI workflows and bring the resulting media directly into the editing workspace instead of jumping between disconnected tools.

▤

Professional timeline

Multi-track media, selection, trimming, splitting, transitions, keyframes, playback, captions and export controls live in one workspace.

⌁

Agents that understand the edit

The local MCP layer exposes supported project and workflow capabilities to compatible AI agents for inspection, planning, preview and approved actions.
